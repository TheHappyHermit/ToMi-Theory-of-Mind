User direction, 2026-09-25: *"don't worry about the things that have to do with radios or hacking or
finance or osint"* — extended to *"if it's about those things, you can skip over reading the file so we
don't even waste the compute reading each of those files because there's a lot of them. But only cut

Second authorization, 2026-09-26: *"the maintenance reports that go from 1247-1257"* — 11 files under

regeneration, link checkers). They describe maintenance *of* the corpus rather than being part of it,
so reading them yields no new knowledge about the subject matter. Excluded on that basis, by line
number, as directed. This is a **separate scope from the four topics above** — it is not a fifth
topic, and it does not widen the four-topic filter.
