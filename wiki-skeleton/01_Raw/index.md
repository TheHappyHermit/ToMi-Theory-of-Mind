# Raw

Part of the active wiki. Immutable intake. Everything enters here first, unclassified. Capturing must never require deciding what something means.

## Sub-folders

- [Web](Web/index.md) — Browser captures and web clippings. Verbatim, never interpreted here.
- [Conversations](Conversations/index.md) — Chat exports and dialogue transcripts.
- [Quick-Captures](Quick-Captures/index.md) — One-line thoughts, not yet thought through. `epistemic: hypothesis` is honest about that rather than asserting it.
- [Documents](Documents/index.md) — Standalone files: PDFs, docs, data dumps.
- [Imports](Imports/index.md) — Bulk imports and migrations from other systems.
- [Attachments](Attachments/index.md) — Media that accompanies other captures: images, audio.
